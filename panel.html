Welcome to the CRM Sidekick!

Before you get started, here’s a quick overview of what this tool is and how it can help you.

CRM Sidekick is a browser extension designed to streamline your daily tasks with a set of helpful, easy-to-use tools. While it’s not a full automation platform like Power Automate and still requires some user interaction, it removes many of the time-consuming steps you deal with every day.

Instead of one-click check-ins, CRM Sidekick guides you through the process and takes care of repetitive work such as:

  • Naming and zipping files
  • Formatting and submitting your CRM notes
  • Eliminating common manual entry tasks

If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to Jordan.
